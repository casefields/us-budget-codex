    Sources: Treasury's FY 2022–2026 September Monthly Treasury Statements: budget functions and totals, subfunctions, and agency and account lines. The supplied screenshots largely show FY 2025, so their numbers are not substituted for FY 2026. See the budget audit and comparisons and screenshot-by-screenshot coverage.

    How lines are built: Function and subfunction totals are Treasury net outlays. The placements of account lines are editorial matches, not an official Treasury account-to-subfunction crosswalk. An unassigned remainder is a calculated reconciliation, not a separately verified account; it may include unmatched spending, receipts, transfers and classification differences. Parent and child lines must not be added together.

    Scenario limits: Cuts hold net interest, offsets and unassigned reconciliation estimates fixed. A positive net line cannot be cut below zero, even when its account payments exceed that net amount. They do not model legal restrictions, the timing of obligations, receipts, economic effects or changes in future interest. Account cuts with corresponding receipts can overstate savings. Warnings identify selected existing commitments and do not establish which cuts are legally possible.
